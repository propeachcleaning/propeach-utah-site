What's Included

Every clean follows our detailed checklist: kitchen and bathrooms cleaned and sanitized, surfaces dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, and trash taken out. Our background-checked cleaners bring their own eco-friendly supplies, and every visit is backed by our 24-hour free touch-up.

Need more detail? See deep cleaning, move-out cleaning or Airbnb cleaning in Salt Lake City.

What Can Change Your Price

  • Home size. Pricing is set by square footage.
  • Type of clean. Deep cleaning adds $50 and move-in/move-out adds $100.
  • Pets. $15 per pet for the extra hair on floors, furniture and baseboards.
  • Extras. Optional add-ons like windows, laundry, organizing or a garage. Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets is already included in every move-out clean.
  • Parking. Paid parking or garage fees at your building are added to the total.

Standard or Deep Clean?

If your home hasn't had a professional cleaning in the last month or two, start with a deep clean. After that, a standard clean on a weekly, biweekly or monthly schedule keeps it fresh.

Salt Lake City Cleaning Cost FAQs

How much does move-out cleaning cost in Salt Lake City?

Move-in/move-out cleaning with Pro Peach Cleaning starts at $249 for homes under 1,000 square feet and goes up about $30 for every additional 250 square feet.

What's included in a move-out clean?

Inside cabinets and drawers, inside the oven, refrigerator and microwave, closets, baseboards, bathrooms, kitchen and all floors.

Does the home need to be empty?

Yes. Move-in/move-out cleaning is priced for empty homes so we can reach inside every cabinet, closet and appliance.
