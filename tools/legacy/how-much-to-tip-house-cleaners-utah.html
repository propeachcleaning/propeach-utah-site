How Much to Tip House Cleaners

Tipping your cleaning team is never required, but it is a thank-you they always appreciate. Here is what many customers in the Salt Lake Valley tip for each type of cleaning.

Get My Price

The Short Answer

There is no rule. Many customers tip 15% to 20% of the cleaning price for a one-time clean, or $10 to $20 per cleaner. Bigger jobs like deep cleans and move-outs often get a little more. Recurring customers usually tip a smaller amount each visit, or give a holiday tip.

Tipping Guide by Type of Cleaning

  • Standard cleaning: 15% to 20%, or $10 to $20 per cleaner
  • Deep cleaning: 15% to 20%, or $20 to $40 per cleaner
  • Move-in or move-out cleaning: 15% to 20%, or $20 to $40 per cleaner
  • Recurring cleaning: a smaller tip each visit, or a holiday tip of about one cleaning
  • Airbnb turnovers: many hosts skip per-visit tips and tip at the holidays

These are common customer habits, not a Pro Peach requirement. Tips are always optional.

When a Bigger Tip Makes Sense

  • The home needed more work than usual, like after a party, a renovation or a long stretch without a cleaning
  • You booked on short notice
  • The team worked through pet hair or a long list of extras
  • Your recurring team keeps going above and beyond
  • It is the holidays

How Tipping Works With Pro Peach

  • Tips are optional. Your cleaning price does not include a tip, and a tip is never required.
  • Add it when you book. Our booking form has a tip option, so you can tip with your card or another digital payment.
  • It goes to your cleaners. 100% of every tip goes to the cleaners who cleaned your home, split across the team.
  • Tip any way you like: when you book, after the clean, or in cash to your cleaner.

Do You Have to Tip House Cleaners?

No. Tipping is a personal choice. Every Pro Peach cleaner is interviewed and background-checked and follows the same detailed checklist on every visit, whether or not you tip.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip a house cleaner?

Many customers tip 15% to 20% of the cleaning price, or $10 to $20 per cleaner for a standard clean. It is always optional.

Should I tip more for a deep clean or move-out clean?

Many customers do, since those cleanings take longer. 15% to 20% of the price, or $20 to $40 per cleaner, is common.

Do I need to tip every time for recurring cleaning?

No. Many recurring customers tip a smaller amount per visit, or skip it and give a holiday tip of about one cleaning.

Is tipping required with Pro Peach Cleaning?

No. Your price does not include a tip and a tip is never required.

Do the cleaners get the tip?

Yes. 100% of every tip goes to the cleaners who cleaned your home, split across the team.
