Airbnb Cleaning Cost in Salt Lake City: What Hosts Pay Per Turnover

Airbnb and vacation rental turnovers with Pro Peach Cleaning start at $149 for rentals under 1,000 square feet, the same as our standard cleaning price. Here is what a turnover costs by rental size, and how to set a cleaning fee that covers it.

Get My Price

Turnover Prices by Rental Size

  • Under 1,000 sq ft: from $149
  • 1,000 to 1,249 sq ft: from $189
  • 1,250 to 1,499 sq ft: from $219
  • 1,500 to 1,749 sq ft: from $249
  • 1,750 to 1,999 sq ft: from $279
  • 2,000 to 2,249 sq ft: from $309
  • 2,250 to 2,499 sq ft: from $339
  • 2,500 to 2,749 sq ft: from $369
  • 2,750 to 2,999 sq ft: from $399
  • 3,000 sq ft and up: quoted for your home

Most condos and townhome rentals near downtown and the canyons fall in the first few tiers.

What Every Turnover Includes

  • Kitchen cleaned and counters, sink and appliance fronts wiped
  • Bathrooms sanitized
  • Beds made and linens changed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and surfaces dusted

Setting Your Cleaning Fee

Your cleaning fee should cover the turnover itself plus anything you restock between guests, like toiletries, paper goods and linens. Guests see the fee before they book, so price it for your real costs and keep your nightly rate competitive.

Ski Season in Salt Lake

From December through spring, rentals near Little and Big Cottonwood Canyons book back to back. Ski boots, wet gear and road salt mean extra floor and entryway work, so plan a seasonal deep clean between busy stretches.

What Can Change Your Price

  • Condition: a rental left in rough shape takes longer
  • Pets: $15 per pet
  • Clutter: more items to move means more time
  • Same-day bookings: add $50
  • Paid parking at the property may be added

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Salt Lake City?

Turnovers start at $149 for rentals under 1,000 square feet and go up about $30 for every additional 250 square feet.

Can you clean between same-day check-out and check-in?

Yes. Share your check-out and check-in times when you book and we schedule the turnover in between.

Do you change the linens?

Yes. We make the beds and change linens as part of every turnover.
