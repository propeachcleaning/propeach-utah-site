Do I Need to Be Home for House Cleaners?

No. Many of our clients in the Salt Lake Valley are at work, out of town or running errands while we clean. Here is how to give the team access and what to set up ahead of time.

Get My Price

Ways to Let the Team In

  • A key left in a lockbox or a safe spot
  • A door code or smart lock code
  • A garage code
  • Gate or community access codes
  • A key left with the front desk or concierge

Share the details in your booking notes and we will follow them every visit.

Condo and Apartment Buildings

In downtown Salt Lake City and other condo buildings, the front desk often decides who goes up. Sort it out the day before:

  • Tell the front desk or concierge a cleaning team is coming and add them to your guest list
  • Leave a key or written authorization if the desk needs one
  • Ask whether vendors need to check in or book the service elevator
  • If your building needs a certificate of insurance, tell us when you book

Before You Leave

  • Secure your pets in a closed room or crate, and tell us which room to skip
  • Put away valuables like jewelry, cash, medications and important documents
  • Leave a short note about what matters most this visit and any surfaces that need special care
  • Leave fresh sheets out if you would like the beds made with clean linens
  • Point out where the team can park

More tips in our guide to preparing for house cleaners.

Rather Be Home? That Works Too

It is fine to be home. Staying out of the room being cleaned helps things move faster. Working from home? Tell the team which room to clean first, or which one to leave alone during your calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients give us a key, lockbox, door code, smart lock or gate access. Just share the instructions when you book.

How do I let cleaners into my condo building?

Tell your front desk or concierge a cleaning team is coming, leave a key or authorization, and ask about vendor check-in or service elevator rules.

What should I do with my pets?

Pets are welcome, but please keep them secured in a closed room or crate during the clean and tell us which room to skip.

Are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes. Every Pro Peach cleaner is interviewed and background-checked, and Pro Peach Cleaning is licensed, bonded and insured.
