  • Thanksgiving: book a deep clean 1 to 3 days before so the kitchen is ready.

  • Ski-season hosts: pair a holiday deep clean with turnover cleaning between guests.

Salt Lake Holiday Cleaning Tips

Snowy boots and road salt are the biggest mess in a Utah holiday home. Put a waterproof tray by every door, wipe salt off hard floors before it dries and dulls them, and keep a towel handy for wet gear. Winter inversion days keep windows closed, so dust and run the bathroom and kitchen fans more often while guests are staying.

Holiday Cleaning FAQs

How far ahead should I book a holiday cleaning?

As early as you can. The weeks before Thanksgiving and Christmas are our busiest, and weekend dates fill first. Book online to see open times.

Is a holiday clean the same as a deep clean?

Yes. Holiday cleaning is our deep cleaning service timed around your event, with extra focus on the kitchen, guest bathrooms and the rooms guests will use.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients give us access with a key, lockbox or door code so they can shop and run errands while we clean.
