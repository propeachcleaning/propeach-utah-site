FAQS - HOW CAN WE HELP

Can I trust my cleaning professional?
All of our cleaners are interviewed and pass a nationwide detailed background check. We do not hire anyone unless they are trustworthy and have a clean record. Only 4% of cleaners make it through the hiring process.
How do I book my first appointment?
You can schedule your first cleaning directly through our website using the 'Book Now' tab. Alternatively, feel free to call or text us at (385) 217-9244 for assistance!
What areas do you serve?
We serve Salt Lake City and the Salt Lake Valley, including Sugar House, Millcreek, Holladay, Murray, Cottonwood Heights and South Salt Lake. We also serve metro Atlanta, Georgia. Contact us to confirm availability for your address.
Are Pro Peach cleaners licensed, insured and background-checked?
Yes. Pro Peach Cleaning is licensed, bonded and insured. Only 4% of cleaners make it through our hiring process, and every cleaner is interviewed and passes a background check before they ever step into your home.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes, we provide all cleaning supplies, products and equipment, unless there is a specific request for a certain service OR if you specifically want us to use your equipment.
What is your 24-hour free touch-up policy?
If we missed a spot, text us at (385) 217-9244 within 24 hours of your cleaning. We'll come back and touch it up for free. Your happiness is our top priority.
Is my billing information kept safe and secure?
We prioritize security with three layers of protection. Our booking page is safeguarded by extended validation SSL. Additionally, our booking form features its own 256-bit security layer. Credit card transactions are securely processed through Stripe, using their own 256-bit security protocol. For added security, we do not store credit card numbers in our system—only a token for charging purposes. Your security is of utmost importance to us. We take security extremely seriously.
What is your reschedule policy?
We understand that unexpected things happen in life.. We just kindly request that you notify us by responding to our reminders at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice will incur a 50% cancellation fee, which compensates our cleaners for their lost work on that day.
