Standard Cleaning

Our standard clean covers all main living areas: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathroom sanitization, kitchen cleaning (countertops, stovetop, exterior appliances), and general tidying.

Deep Cleaning

A deep clean goes beyond the surface — everything in a standard clean plus inside cabinets and appliances, scrubbed tile grout, detailed baseboards, window tracks, and areas not covered in a routine clean.

Move In / Move Out Cleaning

Our moving clean is a comprehensive deep clean for empty properties — all rooms, inside cabinets and drawers, inside all appliances, all bathrooms, window sills and tracks, baseboards, and full floor care.

Commercial Cleaning

We clean offices, restaurants, bars, retail spaces, and more throughout Utah. Contact us and we'll put together a custom cleaning plan built around your schedule and needs.

Which types of commercial spaces do you offer cleaning services for?

Pro Peach Cleaning services a wide range of commercial properties across Utah, including offices, retail stores, gyms, medical offices, educational institutions, and more, tailoring our services to meet your specific needs.

How frequently can you provide commercial cleaning services?

We offer flexible scheduling to fit your business needs, from daily to weekly to monthly services, ensuring your Utah commercial space remains impeccably clean.

What services do we NOT do?

For safety reasons and to protect our team’s health and liability, we do not clean or handle the following: delicate blinds prone to breakage, hoarder homes, biohazards such as feces, urine, vomit, blood, or drug paraphernalia, infestations of bugs or pests, or tasks that require climbing or using second-story ladders.
